A Quality Evaluation of
AutoScholarQuery
To assess the quality of AutoScholarQuery, we sam-
pled 100 query-paper pairs and evaluated the ra-
tionality and relevance of each query and its cor-
responding paper. The detailed evaluation criteria
are as follows:
• A qualified query should be a complete and
understandable sentence. For example, incom-
plete or fragmented sentences are not accept-
able.
• A query that misrepresents the meaning of the
source paper, leading to irrelevant citations,
is not qualified. This includes queries that
exaggerate the scope or introduce incorrect
conditions.
• A query is ambiguous if there is insuffi-
cient context and additional information is
needed. For instance, abbreviations with mul-
tiple meanings can create ambiguity, leading
to the corresponding citations being incom-
plete answer lists.
